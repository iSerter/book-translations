## The 18 Rules of the Bhagavad Gita - According to Claude Opus 4.5

1. If confusion and despair take place, do not flee from life's battles but seek wisdom from a higher source.
2. Know yourself as the eternal soul that cannot be destroyed, and act without being paralyzed by fear of outcomes.
3. Perform your duties with excellence and dedication, but release all attachment to the fruits of your actions.
4. Pursue knowledge and understanding, recognizing that wisdom burns away the bonds of karma like fire turns wood to ash.
5. Whether you act or renounce, let the spirit of inner renunciation guide you—work in the world while remaining untouched by it.
6. Discipline your mind through steady meditation, becoming your own friend rather than your own enemy.
7. See the Divine as both the thread running through all existence and the source from which everything emerges.
8. Remember the Divine at all times, especially at the moment of death, for where your mind rests, there you go.
9. Offer everything you do—eating, giving, practicing—as a humble offering to the Divine.
10. Recognize the Divine presence in all excellence, beauty, and power you encounter in this world.
11. Bow before the infinite mystery that contains all creation and destruction within itself.
12. Devote your heart completely, for sincere love offered to the Divine is the most direct path.
13. Distinguish between the field of experience and the knower of the field, understanding what you truly are.
14. Rise above the three qualities of nature—inertia, passion, and clarity—by witnessing them without identification.
15. Cut through attachment with the weapon of detachment and take refuge in the Supreme Being beyond all change.
16. Cultivate divine qualities like fearlessness, purity, and compassion; abandon the demonic traits of pride, anger, and cruelty.
17. Let your faith, food, worship, and discipline be sattvic—pure, elevating, and in harmony with truth.
18. Surrender completely to the Divine will, abandoning all other supports, and you will be liberated from all sorrow.

## The 18 Rules of the Bhagavad Gita - According to Gemini 3 Pro

1.  **Face inner battles with courage**, refusing to yield to despair, confusion, or the urge to flee.
2.  **Perform duty with perfect equanimity**, grounded in the knowing that the self is the eternal Spirit, not the aging body.
3.  **Act selflessly for the good of the whole**, engaging in the world without claiming the fruits of labor.
4.  **Seek the fire of wisdom**, seeing the stillness within action and the action within stillness.
5.  **Live active but untouched**, resting peacefully like a lotus leaf on water—dry amidst the waves.
6.  **Master the mind through discipline**, transforming it from an enemy into the greatest ally.
7.  **Surrender to the Source**, recognizing the Divine Intelligence sustaining the entire cosmos.
8.  **Practice constant remembrance**, training thoughts to return to the Divine in every moment of life and death.
9.  **Offer every act as sacrifice**, turning every meal, breath, and action into a sacred gift of love to the Supreme.
10. **Perceive the Divine Splendor**, seeing the spark of the Infinite in the beauty and power of all beings.
11. **Serve as a humble instrument**, bowing to the Universal Form that creates, sustains, and dissolves all things.
12. **Anchor the heart in devotion**, bearing no ill will toward any being and remaining balanced in joy or sorrow.
13. **Be the silent Witness**, observing the body and mind as a field of change while remaining the changeless Knower.
14. **Rise above the modes of nature**, observing the flow of energy (gunas) without getting entangled in them.
15. **Sever the roots of attachment**, using the sword of detachment to dwell permanently in the Eternal Abode.
16. **Cultivate divine qualities**, ruthlessly weeding out arrogance, anger, and fear from the heart.
17. **Purify existence**, aligning faith, food, and speech strictly with the vibration of goodness and light.
18. **Renounce the ego of doership**, surrendering completely to the Divine Will to find freedom in total acceptance.
